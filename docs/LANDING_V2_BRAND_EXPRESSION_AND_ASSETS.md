# Landing V2 — Brand Expression & Asset Plan

## A. Brand expression

**Tone.** Operational Editorial: уверенная инженерная точность, показанная через реальные материалы, рабочие поверхности и контролируемый свет. OptiMate выглядит как команда, которая приводит живой бизнес в порядок, а не как абстрактный AI-сервис.

**Visual principles.**

- Cream paper, dark ink and restrained oxide are the connective palette; each image carries the same warm-neutral base and a single muted oxide cue.
- Material scenes stay close, intentional and useful: metal, glass, paper, textile and a working surface instead of generic technology symbolism.
- Product UI remains HTML and primary. Images form atmosphere, scale and a memorable world around the interface.
- Crop is editorial: one legible hero object per frame, generous negative space, controlled lighting and no embedded copy.

**Emotional goals.** Make the page feel composed, human and commercially real: a visitor should sense both premium craft and the operational systems beneath it.

**Avoid.** Purple gradients, neon, cyberpunk, glowing brains/orbs, generic chatbot screens, stock-office smiles, fashion-editorial clichés, overdecorated collage and any text/logotype baked into imagery.
