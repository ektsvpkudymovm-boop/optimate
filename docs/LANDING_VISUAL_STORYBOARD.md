# LANDING VISUAL STORYBOARD

## 03 / COMPANY CONTEXT

### Visual role
Central brand thesis / system manifesto.

### Narrative
Fragmented company knowledge → Unified company context → Information begins to work.

### Composition
Dark conceptual system field.

### Motion
One-shot deterministic reveal.

### Motion intensity
Medium.

### Surface
Dark.

### Primary label
Единый контекст компании.

### Forbidden
Product dashboard, feature cards, central AI orb, neural network, PowerPoint diagram, looping animation.

## 04 / KNOWLEDGE WORKBENCH

### Visual role
Product proof: question → source-backed answer → action.

### Composition
Editorial intro and one focused workbench; mode rail is compact, not a feature-card grid.

### Surface
Light warm working surface.

### Interaction / motion
Manual knowledge-mode selector; static final workflow, safe for reduced motion.

### Mobile strategy
Question, answer, sources, action and mode rail become one readable vertical sequence.

### Forbidden
ChatGPT imitation, full hero app shell, four large capability cards.

## 05 / CONVERSATION ANALYSIS

### Visual role
Transformation: conversation → understanding → business action.

### Composition
Temporal transcript, extraction column and action column; compact capability rail below.

### Surface
Warm subtle.

### Interaction / motion
Manual capability selection; no waveform loop.

### Mobile strategy
Transcript, understanding and actions stack in chronological order.

### Forbidden
Decorative waveform and repeated knowledge-workbench layout.

## 06 / MARKET INSIGHTS

### Visual role
Insight expansion: multiple communications become a repeatable signal.

### Composition
Evidence rows converge on one selected management interpretation.

### Surface
Dark evidence field.

### Interaction / motion
Manual purchasing/assortment/marketing selector; no fake metrics.

### Mobile strategy
Evidence then arrow then conclusion, vertically.

### Forbidden
Charts without source data, KPI or ROI claims.

## 07 / AUTOMATION ARCHITECTURE

### Surface
Light.

### Mobile strategy
One vertical chain with feedback marker.

### Forbidden
Four unrelated cards.
